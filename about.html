About Us

A little witchy. A little weird. Always a mystery.

Ravens Scoops started with the three of us—a mother and two daughters who have always shared a love for anything macabre, paranormal, metaphysical, and true crime. We love wandering through metaphysical shops, finding weird and unusual things, and, of course, we all love a good surprise.

That love for the strange, mysterious, and unexpected is really where Ravens Scoops came from. We wanted to create something together that combined all the things we enjoy—from crystals and witchy finds to spooky, macabre, paranormal, true crime, and sometimes just cute or completely random things that make us smile.

And because we love surprises ourselves, mystery is a huge part of what we do. With our mystery ball bundles, you choose how many picks you want and let the raven decide what you get. Half the fun is not knowing what's coming until you open it.

Ravens Scoops is something we're building together as a family, and we're excited to share a little bit of our weirdness with everyone who loves these things as much as we do.

Let the raven choose. 🖤
