Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question about Ravens Scoops? Here are answers to some of our most frequently asked questions.

What is your turnaround time?

Our turnaround time is typically 1–3 weeks because each Ravens Scoops order is individually hand-picked and curated. We strive to have orders prepared and shipped within 7 days of ordering, but please allow up to 3 weeks for your order to be prepared.

How much is shipping?

Shipping costs are based on your location and will be calculated during checkout.

Where do you ship?

Ravens Scoops currently ships within the United States only. At this time, we do not offer international shipping.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Due to the nature of our mystery bundles, we do not accept returns or exchanges because of an item you do not like or would not have personally chosen. The mystery is part of the Ravens Scoops experience, and the items inside are selected at random.

Can I request specific items?

No. We are unable to accommodate requests for specific items. Each bundle is individually hand-picked and curated while still keeping the contents a mystery.

Will every mystery bundle be different?

We strive to make every mystery bundle different and provide a unique experience with each order. However, because our inventory changes and items are selected from what is available, we cannot guarantee that you will never receive a repeat item, especially across multiple orders.

What if an item arrives damaged or is missing from my order?

If an item arrives damaged or you believe something is missing from your order, please contact Ravens Scoops at ravensscoops@gmail.com. Please include your order information and details about the issue so we can look into it and help.

What if my package is lost or delayed during shipping?

Once your order has shipped, Ravens Scoops has access to the same carrier tracking information available to you. If your tracking shows a shipping delay, delivery issue, or a package that may be lost, please contact the shipping carrier directly for assistance.

Still Have a Question?

We're happy to help. Contact Ravens Scoops at ravensscoops@gmail.com .
